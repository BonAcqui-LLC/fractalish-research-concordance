# 1. CURRENT CF PHYSICAL GRAMMAR

## 1.1 Becoming is the only primitive currently admitted

The current owner position is:

> **Becoming is the only constant in CF and the only primitive.**

CF therefore does not begin by declaring a primitive object, state, particle, field, relation, system, transition token, observer, or clock.

This is a **theory commitment**, not an experimentally established theorem. It is chosen because it provides the smallest current owner grammar that does not reinsert the categorical divisions CF is trying to test.

The existence of an inaccessible pre-developed or zero condition is treated separately in §2.

## 1.2 Formation and morphology

**Formation** names the ongoing activity of becoming as it is physically expressed.  
**Morphology** names the observable organization / arrangement / manifestation that formation presents at a chosen resolution.

These are not two substances. Morphology is formation made observable at a given resolution; existing morphology then participates in further formation.

A useful non-foundational algorithmic compression is:

```text
1. becoming / perturbation / state-change occurs;
2. the whole present formation participates in the resolution;
3. a morphology or morphological change is expressed;
4. that morphology is now part of the condition of subsequent formation;
5. repeat.
```

At the Wall (§2), step 1 need not presuppose an interaction between two already-existing objects. The first conceptual departure may be only a state change of unknown mechanism.

## 1.3 No retention requirement

A morphology does not have to persist to count as formation.

A change may:

- persist;
- scar another morphology;
- be recruited later;
- decay into other formation;
- terminate with no detectable downstream consequence;
- or remain below our current ability to detect its consequence.

Therefore the older public compression:

```text
change -> retained consequence -> altered constraint -> altered reachability
```

is still useful for **history-bearing formation**, but it is not the complete universal CF grammar. Retention is an optional development, not an admission ticket.

## 1.4 Morphology is both manifestation and condition

A central CF relation is:

> **Formation writes morphology; morphology conditions formation.**

This is not meant as a two-node feedback loop isolated from the rest of reality. It is a compact way to say that whatever organization presently exists is physically included in the next negotiation.

A scar, crystal lattice, stress pattern, channel, branch, phase distribution, memory substrate, organismal structure, geological layer, trained weight pattern, institution, or engineered tool can all be studied as morphology at different resolutions. Whether any one of these carries durable history must be measured rather than assumed.

## 1.5 Negotiation is non-agentic shorthand

CF often uses **negotiation** because it captures the fact that no single participant gets unilateral authorship of the result. It does not imply consciousness, intention, bargaining, or purpose.

The word means approximately:

> **the resolved morphology is jointly conditioned by the formations, gradients, couplings, constraints, and perturbations actually present.**

## 1.6 Consequence is broader than retained consequence

“Consequence” in CF does not mean only a durable downstream effect. The immediate physical distinction registered in morphology can be consequential even if it later disappears.

When the distinction persists and changes later negotiation, it becomes **operative history**. When it does not persist, the theory does not invent a hidden memory merely to preserve the chain.

## 1.7 Logical progression is the falsification spine

CF makes a stronger claim than “things happen and then other things happen.” At any declared resolution, the resulting morphology must be physically consequent upon the formative condition in which it is expressed, and that morphology remains part of the developed condition thereafter unless it is physically transformed / redistributed / terminated.

A bookkeeping compression is:

```text
F_n -> M_n
M_n is physically included in the developed condition of F_(n+1)
repeat
```

The arrows remain notation, not travelers or a fundamental temporal conveyor.

A **genuine break** would be an experimentally established case in which:

- a morphology is not consequent upon the physically participating formative condition;
- a new causal primitive must be inserted at a conventional domain boundary;
- or a physically complete formative condition is demonstrated to be insufficient in principle for the realized morphology.

If such a break is established, CF must narrow or fail. Missing measurements, unresolved mechanism, computational difficulty, or a gap label do not by themselves constitute a break; they remain `HOLD` until the formative link is known or shown impossible.

## 1.8 Simple kernel, complex realization

CF does not require mathematical novelty for its own sake. The kernel may remain extremely small while realized formations are combinatorially and computationally difficult.

The formalization rule is:

> **Add complexity where the receipts require complexity; never add a new causal primitive merely to decorate an unresolved link.**

Native variables, field equations, conservation relations, boundary / initial conditions, stochastic descriptions, numerical methods, coarse-graining, and domain-specific mathematics may all remain necessary tools. Their use does not automatically make them separate ontological engines.

# 5. GRADIENT, “LEVEL,” ACCUMULATION, AND DECAY

## 5.1 “Everything seeks level” — CF generalization

The everyday statement “water seeks level” is retained as an intuitive entry point, not as a literal universal law.

The CF generalization is:

> **An unresolved difference continues to participate in formation wherever an admissible route for relaxation or redistribution remains.**

“Level” therefore does not mean “identical everywhere.” It means that a gradient remains active until the present formation no longer permits that gradient to resolve further along that route.

## 5.2 Constrained sorting, not featureless sameness

A settled system can remain highly stratified. Density, phase, chemistry, temperature, pressure, rotation, charge, fields, geometry, and other constraints can support persistent gradients.

The useful analogy is material settling into a layered gradient in a beaker: the endpoint is **constrained sorting**, not homogeneous blankness.

## 5.3 Gravity as a persistent large-mass relaxation bias

For a large gravitating mass such as Earth, gravity supplies a persistent directional bias toward lower gravitational potential under the conditions described by native physics.

Loose relief slumps; sediment settles; denser material can migrate inward under appropriate conditions; lighter material can occupy outward regions; unsupported structures erode or collapse. Other forces and constraints can maintain substantial non-level morphology for long periods.

CF uses this as a major visible example of the wider gradient / relaxation principle, not as proof that every gradient is “gravity.”

## 5.4 Formation and return

A current owner compression is:

```text
formation / generative reorganization
    <-> maintained gradients / morphology
    <-> decay / release / redistribution
    <-> further available formation
```

This is not a fundamental binary and should not be frozen into two opposing substances. At one resolution a process may look like decay while simultaneously contributing to formation elsewhere.

## 5.5 Decay is not mere disappearance

Decay is a central CF signal because it frequently exposes what had been holding a morphology together and releases material / gradients / constraints into further negotiation.

The current owner usage is broader than radioactive decay. It includes loss, relaxation, erosion, decomposition, organizational breakdown, dissipation, de-binding, and other cases where maintained morphology loses constraint and becomes available to subsequent formation.

## 5.6 Energy language guardrail

The owner intuition is that the generative / return cycle is an engine of formation. For scientific use, state it carefully:

- decay does **not create energy from nothing**;
- relaxation can release or redistribute energy that had been stored in gravitational, chemical, elastic, phase, electromagnetic, nuclear, or other native structures;
- solar flux supplies a major continuing external energy input at Earth's surface;
- gravitational infall / rearrangement can release energy under conventional physics;
- CF's proposed contribution is the **formation interpretation**, not a repeal of conservation.

## 5.7 Accumulation and deprivation — Entroresilience lineage

The older Entroresilience language of **peaks / valleys** and **accumulation / deprivation** remains important to the current theory lineage.

The key idea is not that every social or physical system obeys one scalar equation. It is that an accumulation is never causally isolated from the relative deprivation / gradient that accompanies it inside the larger formation.

> **The valley is not separate from the spike.**

Human systems can amplify disturbances by deliberately locking accumulations, blocking return routes, or narrowing the admissible paths by which gradients would otherwise reorganize. This is a general CF observation about intervention and constraint, not merely an environmental or political claim.

# 11. CF-LOGIC / RESEARCH GOVERNANCE

The following controls survive the earlier restoration and remain mandatory.

## 11.1 Consensus is not evidence

Agreement among AI systems, researchers, or documents can help locate convergence. It does not promote a physical claim by itself.

## 11.2 Native-domain authority

When CF touches GR, quantum theory, materials science, chemistry, biology, neuroscience, control theory, information theory, or another mature domain, the native measurements and equations remain authoritative within their demonstrated scope.

CF may reorganize the question. It does not get to ignore the receipts.

## 11.3 Hot assimilation, cold promotion

New ideas may enter the research memory quickly. Promotion into the physical spine must be slow, explicit, and receipt-bearing.

## 11.4 Freeze before reveal

Whenever a dataset / experiment can adjudicate a prediction:

1. freeze the claim;
2. freeze variables and tolerances;
3. freeze the baseline;
4. protect adjudicating evidence from the prediction-making process;
5. run once;
6. preserve positive, null, contradictory, and closure results.

## 11.5 Record is not runtime

A ledger, graph, embedding, narrative, database, or provenance object that successfully describes formation is not automatically the mechanism that executed it.

## 11.6 Time firewall

CF may use clock time, proper time, rates, durations, phases, chronology, ordering, and native temporal parameters.
Do not smuggle a flowing time-substance back into the causal story merely by using words such as “then,” “travels,” “moves,” or “propagates.” State the measured office that the temporal parameter performs.

## 11.7 Conservation firewall

No CF interpretation licenses violation of conservation laws. “Not cargo” is a representational point, not permission to lose energy, momentum, charge, angular momentum, or other conserved quantities.

## 11.8 Typed correspondence

For cross-domain comparisons, declare:

- source and target domains;
- resolution / coarse-graining;
- variables and units;
- intervention family;
- horizon / window;
- what structure is claimed to correspond;
- what breaks;
- evidence;
- and uncertainty / error.

Use `INCOMPARABLE` or `UNSUPPORTED` when appropriate.

## 11.9 Preserve negative results

Nulls, failed transfers, broken analogies, snapshot closure, failed compression, contradictory evidence, and superseded owner formulations remain part of the permanent research history.

## 11.10 Receipt / ontology firewall

When CF enters an established domain, separate:

```text
OBSERVATION / MEASUREMENT / SUCCESSFUL QUANTITATIVE RELATION
from
INTERPRETATION / ONTOLOGICAL STORY ABOUT THAT RECEIPT
```

CF owes the first. It does not automatically owe the second.

For example, a CF gravity account must recover the successful gravitational receipt family; it is not required in advance to treat every interpretive phrase used around the native formalism as literal causal furniture. The same rule applies in quantum physics and every other domain.

This is not permission to discard native science. A CF reconstruction that changes the measurements, conservation ledger, or demonstrated quantitative relations has failed unless it earns a better empirical account.

## 11.11 Placeholder causality prohibition / gap-word quarantine

Terms such as `idiopathic`, `spontaneous`, `emergent`, `random`, `stochastic`, `anomalous`, `noise`, `innate`, `instinct`, `self-organizing`, `complex`, `multifactorial`, `primary`, `essential`, `cryptogenic`, `dark`, `collapse`, `information`, `entropy`, `selection`, or similar words may be technically legitimate.

They may **not** satisfy a causal dependency merely because they name the place where understanding stops.

Operational test:

> **If replacing the term with “an unresolved process occurs here” leaves the causal explanation substantially unchanged, the formative link remains open.**

The compiler / research ledger should record:

```text
FORMATION DEBT
HOLD — FORMATIVE LINK UNRESOLVED
```

until the word is cashed out into a measurable mechanism, relation, or declared unknown.

## 11.12 Complete-condition breaker

The strongest current owner hypothesis is that a physically complete formative condition is sufficient for what forms from it. This must be attacked, not assumed.

A serious breaker would establish:

```text
F_a == F_b   in every physically participating respect
but
M_a != M_b
```

with no missing physical distinction capable in principle of accounting for the divergence.

Statistical unpredictability or a probabilistic formalism does not by itself establish this result; nor may CF simply declare every hidden distinction real to protect itself. The burden is symmetrical: define what “physically complete” could mean, then expose the claim to experiment.

Collect formation series in which composition and environmental variables are controlled. Quantify morphology. Hide recipe labels. Test whether a model can infer:

Freeze a formation recipe and predict a distribution / family of morphological observables before the protected run.

- loss of morphological integrity;
- redistribution pathways;
- released energy in native units;

Continue mining / designing experiments that perturb material organization under altered gravitational conditions without assuming in advance that the aim is merely to “prove gravity changes.” The stronger question is whether conventional gravitational description exhausts the relation between perturbation, morphology, and whole-formation gradient.
