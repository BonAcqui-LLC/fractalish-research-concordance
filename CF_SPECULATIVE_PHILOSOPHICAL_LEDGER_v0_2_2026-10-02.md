## 0. WHY THIS LEDGER EXISTS

The Primary Removal Ledger answers:

> **What did a hostile subtraction round actually earn, kill, demote, or leave open?**

The Foundation Extension Ledger answers:

> **What experimental or reconstructive program became useful enough to carry forward?**

Neither ledger is designed to preserve the full speculative landscape.

That creates a serious continuity failure. An idea can be scientifically unearned, philosophically provocative, later demoted, or temporarily unfalsifiable and still be historically essential to understanding how CF developed and where future work may return.

Therefore:

> **ABSENCE FROM THE EARNED LEDGER MUST NEVER MEAN DELETION FROM RESEARCH MEMORY.**

This ledger preserves the idea-space without granting it authority.

A future model should be able to reconstruct not only what CF currently permits itself to claim, but also:

- what James and Melissa suspected;
- what the team explored;
- what was deliberately held open;
- what later work narrowed;
- what metaphors failed but taught something;
- what branches remain possible;
- and which speculative questions motivated the experiments.

This is not a backup of prose. It is a **provenance-preserving map of unearned thought**.

## 1. STATUS TAXONOMY

Every entry receives one primary status.

### ACTIVE WORKING HYPOTHESIS
A speculative claim currently worth testing and compatible with the earned spine, but not yet experimentally promoted.

### UNRESOLVED BRANCH
One of multiple live explanations or ontological readings that current receipts do not discriminate.

### PHILOSOPHICAL POSSIBILITY
A coherent interpretation important to CF's worldview or research motivation that presently lacks an empirical admission route.

### HISTORICAL SCAR
A former CF claim, metaphor, or framing that has been demoted, contradicted, or superseded but must remain visible because later theory was shaped by its failure.

### PROHIBITED CURRENT CLAIM
A statement CF must not presently make as fact. It is preserved because the temptation to make it, or the path by which it arose, is part of the research history.

### APPLICATION SPECULATION
A downstream machine, engineering, social, or cognitive interpretation motivated by CF but not constitutive of the physical theory.

**Status:** ACTIVE WORKING HYPOTHESIS  
**Provenance:** FILE-BACKED
