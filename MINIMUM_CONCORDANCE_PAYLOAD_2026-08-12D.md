## Fledgling Fractalish AI — explicit architecture intent

Current status: **DESIGN INTENT / HOSTING AND INTEGRATION NOT YET ESTABLISHED / END-TO-END FUNCTION NOT CLAIMED**.

After cloud/GPU hosting exists, the plan is **not** to make frontier-scale parameter count the primary objective or to assume the system must internalize the entire accessible knowledge library into local weights.

The intended architecture is a continuity-bearing intelligence that:

- can reason locally;
- knows how and when to call external LLMs as replaceable cognitive instruments;
- can access the internet and external corpora as libraries/sensors;
- retains provenance for sources, model/tool contributions, and conclusions;
- reconstructs causal neighborhoods rather than merely retrieving documents;
- carries learned lineage across sessions, interruptions, host changes, and external-model substitutions;
- makes failures cumulative;
- incorporates verified lessons through R2R;
- reduces future compute required to correctly reuse prior understanding;
- preserves HOLD and irrecoverable-provenance states rather than forcing closure.

This is not parameter-count dogma. Parameterized local components may be used when useful; the program simply does not treat parameter mass as the governing intelligence metric.

## Reasoning capacity versus continuity custody

The architecture explicitly separates:

- **reasoning capacity** — potentially supplied in part by external frontier models and other tools;
- **continuity custody** — the system's retained lineage, causal memory, provenance, contradiction structure, and learned constraints.

External models should be replaceable without erasing the system's prior verified learning.

Core compression:

> **Reasoning can be rented. Continuity cannot.**

## Continuous learning with R2R control

The desired daily/hourly/moment-to-moment learning cadence is reconciled with R2R governance through two rates:

### Hot layer

Continuous source intake, observation, external-model consultation, candidate formation, provisional relational updates, contradiction detection, and working-state change.

### Cold layer

Frozen baseline/falsifier, provenance, independent checking, explicit disposition, receipts, and promotion into durable/canonical continuity memory.

Governing rule remains:

> **Hot assimilation, cold promotion.**

Continuous exposure is not continuous canonization.

## SERA / success metric update

The fledgling AI should be judged on retained useful consequence rather than raw FLOPs or parameter count alone.

Priority measures include:

- verified new understanding per unit compute/energy;
- future search-space reduction per unit compute;
- reduction in compute required for correct governed reuse;
- retry and false-commitment reduction;
- HOLD quality;
- continuity across host/model substitution;
- preservation of revision freedom after consolidation.

A candidate high-level benchmark is:

`Memory Gain = 1 - (cost of correct governed reuse / cost of first acquisition)`

provided the reuse task requires real application/generalization rather than rote replay.
