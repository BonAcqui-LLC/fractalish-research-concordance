# Sequence–Snapshot Divergence Research Plan — 2026-08-12

**Status:** PROPOSED / NOT ESTABLISHED

## Central question

Given the same declared observable snapshot now, does the sequence by which a system arrived there constrain what can happen next?

`P(Y | S, U, H)` versus `P(Y | S, U)`

- `H`: formation/history sequence
- `S`: present measured snapshot
- `U`: novel intervention
- `Y`: subsequent behavior

The discriminating question is whether knowledge of prior sequence improves a preregistered future prediction after the present snapshot has been matched under a frozen finite state description.

## Allowed outcomes

- `0|0 — SNAPSHOT CLOSURE`: progressive state augmentation removes the history-dependent divergence. The causal historical information has been localized into measurable present state.
- `0|1 — RESIDUAL SEQUENCE DIVERGENCE`: a preregistered directional future-response difference survives the frozen snapshot and reasonable predeclared state augmentation. This establishes a residual under the declared measurement model, not a new ontology.

## Reusable assay

1. Create two reproducible, meaningfully different histories `H_A` and `H_B`.
2. Define and freeze a native-motivated finite state vector `S_N`, measurement procedure, matching tolerances, exclusions, and uncertainty.
3. Freeze a novel perturbation `U`, primary response `Y`, native baseline, directional prediction, and falsification condition before execution.
4. Apply identical `U` to the matched cohorts and compare `P(Y | S_N,U,H_A)` with `P(Y | S_N,U,H_B)`.
5. If a residual appears, use successor-preregistered state augmentation to locate the smallest present-state carrier rather than claiming a new grammar.
6. Once a candidate carrier is identified, use controlled subtraction of supporting conditions and record the first transition after each removal to locate where the predictive organization resides.
7. Restore only the minimum required conditions and test whether the predictive organization returns without replaying the entire formation sequence.

## Preferred first proving ground

Controlled materials/path-dependent mechanics is the current candidate because it offers mature native theory, controllable histories, direct measurement, strong perturbation tests, and fewer hidden variables than biology. Candidate implementations include dislocation structure, path-dependent yield, fatigue, and related mechanical systems.

This is not yet a frozen experiment. The selected system must provide two reproducible histories, a defensible finite present-state description, an established native baseline, a novel intervention, a directional prediction, and feasible localization of the causal residue.

## Relationship to ANF

ANF concepts remain candidate explanatory structures, not evidence. The assay is intended to test rather than assume them:

- RETAIN/memory: identify what historical consequence remains causally available.
- Admissibility/reachability: test whether matched snapshots support different futures.
- Failure as formation: test whether failed attempts alter subsequent reachability only when some consequence persists.
- Invitation versus prescription: compare formation histories using recovery, maintenance, and reconstruction measures rather than assuming superiority.
- Constraint Debt: operationalize only if matched outcomes show measurable differences in continuing correction, maintenance burden, residual stress, or recovery.
- Natural/synthetic provenance: test whether distinct histories close at `0|0` or preserve measurable higher-order/future-response differences at `0|1`.
- Trial by Fire: controlled subtraction and minimal reconstruction localize the causal carrier.

No ANF term receives evidentiary promotion merely because it describes the assay.

## Long-range motivation — non-evidentiary

A motivating intuition is preserved without promotion to fact: human reasoning can be viewed partly as repeated decisions under incomplete information, with present judgment shaped by accumulated experience, available knowledge, constraints, incentives, and prior interpretations. Enlarging accessible knowledge libraries may enlarge the set of meaningful choices available to people and machines.

This does not establish that education automatically creates wisdom or prosocial behavior. The narrower objective is to make higher-quality causal understanding easier to reach, harder to monopolize, cumulative across prior failures, and available to subsequent minds without requiring them to repay the full cost of earlier discovery.

Long-range question: **How can accumulated consequence become accumulated understanding without becoming accumulated control?**
