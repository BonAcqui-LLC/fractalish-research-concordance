## H-08 — UNIVERSAL RECIPROCITY / “ONE ANOTHER”

The Rethinking Thinking rounds correctly broke the attempt to use reciprocity as a universal replacement for all causation. Preserve reciprocal closure where measured; do not make it the primitive grammar.

## H-09 — ACCESSIBILITY AS ONE UNIVERSAL SCALAR
